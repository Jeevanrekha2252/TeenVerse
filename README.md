# TeenVerse – AI-Powered Digital Well-being & Life Skills Ecosystem

TeenVerse is an AI-powered digital platform designed to support teenagers in their overall personal development and digital well-being.

## Problem Statement

Today's teenagers face challenges such as academic pressure, mental stress, excessive screen time, cyberbullying, career uncertainty, financial illiteracy and a lack of practical life skills.

TeenVerse aims to provide an integrated platform that combines digital well-being, AI-powered guidance, career exploration, financial literacy, life skills and gamified learning.

## Key Modules

- AI Wellness Companion
- Digital Detox Tracker
- Career Discovery
- Financial Literacy
- Life Skills Learning
- Cyber Safety Education
- Goal Tracking
- Gamification
- Personalized Recommendations
- Mentor and Trusted Resource Support
